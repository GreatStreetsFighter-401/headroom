Moving it pins that assumption at the value you choose, in place of its sampled range, and runs the whole model again on the same draw stream, so the band that comes back is the remaining uncertainty given your choice. Statutory values and the handbook's benchmarks are not sliders: the page cannot change the law. The “why” link opens the entry's source and justification.
